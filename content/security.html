HIPAA compliant

We meet HIPAA requirements for the patient data we handle, and every practice signs a Business Associate Agreement with us before any patient data is shared.

SOC 2 Type II

Our security controls are independently audited against the SOC 2 Type II standard.

Clinician control

Your clinicians set the protocols, the red flags and who gets an escalation.

How patient data is handled

  • Minimum necessary. We use only the patient information needed to run the programs you choose.
  • Encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Access controls with least-privilege access and logging.
  • Call recordings and notes are kept for your audit trail and available to your team.
  • No patient data through the website. Our demo form asks for practice details only.

Safety on every call

  • The coordinator says it's an AI at the start of every call.
  • Patients can ask for a person at any time.
  • Red-flag symptoms you define trigger your escalation protocol in real time, including a warm transfer to your on-call clinician or an instruction to call 911.
  • The coordinator doesn't diagnose, change medications or give clinical advice. Those go to your team.

Frequently asked questions

Will you sign a BAA?

Yes. We sign a Business Associate Agreement with every practice before any patient data is shared.

Does the AI give medical advice?

No. It checks in, collects information, handles routine requests and routes anything clinical to your team.
