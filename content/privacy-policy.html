This policy explains how this website handles information you submit through it. It does not cover patient information handled under a Business Associate Agreement with a practice; that is governed by the agreement.

What we collect

  • Information you submit through the demo form: your name, role, work email, phone, practice name, state, approximate patient volume, programs of interest and EHR.
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited and browser type, collected through standard server logs and analytics.

Please don't submit patient information through this website.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, schedule a demo and send related information
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it

We don't sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information with service providers that help us run the website and our business, such as hosting, email and customer relationship tools, under agreements that limit their use of it.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you submitted, or to stop sending you emails, by replying to any email from us.
