What transitional care management requires

Transitional Care Management (TCM) pays practices for the 30 days after a patient leaves an inpatient hospital, skilled nursing facility or similar setting and returns to the community. It has three parts:

  1. An interactive contact within two business days of discharge. This can be by phone, email or in person, with the patient or caregiver.
  2. Non-face-to-face services, such as reviewing discharge information, following up on pending tests and medication reconciliation.
  3. A face-to-face visit within 7 or 14 days, depending on the code.

TCM billing codes

CodeMedical decision makingFace-to-face visit
99495At least moderate complexityWithin 14 calendar days of discharge
99496High complexityWithin 7 calendar days of discharge

Where TCM breaks down

The two-business-day contact is the step most often missed. Discharge notices arrive late or in a fax queue, staff call once and get voicemail, and by the time someone reaches the patient the window has closed. The visit doesn't get booked in time either, and the patient is at higher risk of going back to the hospital.

What an AI care coordinator does for TCM

  • Calls the same day the discharge comes in, and keeps trying by phone and text until it reaches the patient or caregiver, all inside the two-business-day window.
  • Checks how the patient is doing at home: symptoms, new medications, whether prescriptions were filled, and whether they have what they need.
  • Books the follow-up visit inside the 7- or 14-day window.
  • Escalates red flags such as shortness of breath or chest pain to your on-call clinician right away.
  • Documents the contact date and time, what was discussed and what needs clinical follow-up, for your practitioner to review.

Frequently asked questions

How soon after discharge do you need to contact the patient for TCM?

Within two business days of discharge. The contact can be by phone, email or in person with the patient or their caregiver.

What is the difference between 99495 and 99496?

99495 requires at least moderate complexity medical decision making and a face-to-face visit within 14 days. 99496 requires high complexity decision making and a visit within 7 days.

Can an AI make the TCM contact call?

An AI care coordinator can reach the patient quickly, check on them, book the visit and document the conversation, with clinical questions routed to your staff. Your practitioner remains responsible for the TCM service and the visit. Have your compliance advisor confirm how the contact is documented.
