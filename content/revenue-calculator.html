How the estimate works

The calculator multiplies the patients you enroll at each APCM level by that level's monthly rate:

  • Enrolled patients = Medicare patients × share who enroll
  • G0557 and G0558 patients = enrolled patients with two or more chronic conditions, split by Qualified Medicare Beneficiary (QMB) status
  • G0556 patients = everyone else enrolled
  • Monthly revenue = patients at each level × that level's rate

The default rates are approximate 2025 national averages. Your actual payment depends on your Medicare locality, the year's fee schedule and patient cost sharing. Use the CMS Physician Fee Schedule lookup for your exact rates and enter them above.

What changes the number most

  • Enrollment rate. Every patient has to consent. Reaching every eligible patient, more than once if needed, is what moves this number.
  • Your patient mix. Practices with older, more complex panels have more patients at G0557 and G0558.
  • Keeping patients enrolled. APCM is billed monthly, so patients who drop out stop generating revenue. Regular, useful check-ins keep them in the program.

Frequently asked questions

How much does APCM pay per patient?

Roughly $15 a month for G0556, $50 for G0557 and $107 for G0558, based on 2025 national averages. Rates vary by locality and year.

How many patients enroll in APCM?

It depends on how many eligible patients you reach and how well the program is explained. Patients must consent, and cost sharing may apply.
