The short answer

Chronic Care Management (CCM) pays for minutes of time spent by clinical staff or the billing practitioner. Time spent by software isn't clinical staff time, so an AI call doesn't add billable CCM minutes. Advanced Primary Care Management (APCM) pays for managing the patient each month with no minutes to count. That's why we automate APCM, not CCM.

How CCM is billed

CCM is a Medicare program for patients with two or more chronic conditions expected to last at least 12 months, or until death, that put the patient at significant risk of death, acute exacerbation or functional decline. Each code needs a minimum amount of time in the calendar month.

CodeWhat it coversTime
99490Non-complex CCM by clinical staff, directed by a physician or other qualified practitionerFirst 20 minutes per month
99439Each additional 20 minutes of clinical staff time (add-on to 99490)+20 minutes
99491Non-complex CCM personally provided by the physician or qualified practitioner30 minutes
99487Complex CCM, with moderate or high complexity medical decision making60 minutes
99489Each additional 30 minutes of complex CCM (add-on to 99487)+30 minutes

Rates vary by locality and year. For 99490, the national average has been roughly $60 a month.

Why automation doesn't help CCM revenue

Because CCM pays for staff minutes, a CCM program grows only as fast as you can hire nurses and medical assistants to spend those minutes. Software can make your staff faster at reaching patients and writing notes, but it can't produce the minutes themselves. More automation in CCM means less staff time per patient, not more billing.

The same practitioner can bill APCM or CCM for a patient in a month, not both, and can choose which one each month. Many practices move most CCM patients to APCM and keep CCM for the few complex patients their nurses already spend a lot of time on.

Frequently asked questions

Can an AI care coordinator bill CCM minutes?

No. CCM time is time spent by clinical staff or the billing practitioner. Calls made by software don't count toward the 20 minutes. That's why we run APCM, which has no time requirement.

Can we bill APCM and CCM for the same patient?

Not in the same month by the same practitioner. You choose which one to bill for that patient each month, and a patient can be on CCM one month and APCM the next.

How many minutes do you need to bill CCM?

At least 20 minutes of clinical staff care management time in the calendar month for 99490, 30 minutes of the practitioner's own time for 99491, or 60 minutes for complex CCM (99487).

Who qualifies for chronic care management?

Medicare patients with two or more chronic conditions expected to last at least 12 months, or until death, that put them at significant risk of death, acute exacerbation or functional decline.
